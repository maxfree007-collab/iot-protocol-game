    Important teaching note: the five-layer IoT architecture is a conceptual model, and classifications can vary by textbook. Here, BLE, Zigbee and LoRaWAN are treated as connectivity technologies in the Transport layer; MQTT, CoAP, HTTP and AMQP are application-layer messaging/application protocols. The Business layer contains management and decision processes, not communication protocols.
